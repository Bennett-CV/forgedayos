Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 12, 2026

Overview

Forgeday ("we", "us", or "our") operates the Forgeday mobile and web application (the "Service"). This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have.

Information We Collect

Account information: your name and email address when you register.

Profile and health data: details you enter such as age, gender, weight, height, activity level, nutrition goals, meals, workouts, body weight, journal entries, and financial transactions you choose to log.

Usage data: information about how you interact with the Service, including device and app analytics from our host.

How We Use Your Information

We use your information to provide, personalize, and improve the Service; calculate nutrition targets, momentum points, and performance summaries; generate weekly reviews and insights; communicate with you about your account; and comply with legal obligations.

Data Sharing

Forgeday is built and hosted on Base44, and your data is stored on Base44 infrastructure. We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it with data brokers or advertisers. We may share data with service providers that help us operate the Service (such as hosting, product analytics, and AI providers used for features like weekly review) under appropriate confidentiality obligations, and where required by law.

Health and Financial Data

The app does not use HealthKit and does not read or write Apple Health data. The Finance section is tracking-only: we do not process payments in the app and do not collect or store payment card details.

Data Retention and Your Rights

We retain your information for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide the Service. You may request deletion of your account and associated data at any time from Settings. Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have rights to access, correct, export, or delete your personal information.

Children's Privacy

The Service is not intended for children under 13 (or the minimum age in your jurisdiction). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Contact

Questions: use the contact options on the Settings page in the app. Also see our Terms of Use.
